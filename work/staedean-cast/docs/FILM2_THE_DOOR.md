# FILM 2: THE DOOR

About 49 seconds including the logo card. The follow-up to Mount Staedean, showing how we want to work instead: close to the customer, failing fast, launching sooner.

## The point

Ideas should go to the customer, not up a mountain. Small weekly visits, quick failures, and a handshake when it is good. Then everyone ships it together.

## The story in one paragraph

Back in the office, with Mount Staedean still in the window, the PM gets a new idea. Next to the window is a small teal door: "INNOVATION / CUSTOMER 20 m". He and the Maker walk through it. Week 1 the customer gives a thumbs-down and the prototype goes in the LEARNED bin. Week 2 he points at what to fix and the Maker hammers. Week 3 it lights up and he gives a thumbs-up. They shake hands: "When can I have it?". Week 4 a catapult throws the product over Mount Staedean and it lands in the customer's arms. A shop opens with everyone from the climb behind the counter, department plaques underneath, serving a queue of customers, while the old bench sits empty with a cobweb. Colleagues walk through the door with their own ideas and the sign flips to "HOW WE WORK". End cards: "Some ideas still die. But faster." (the bulb goes out) then "Some ideas live forever, together." (a string of lights), then the STAEDEAN logo with the lights above it.
