# MOUNT STAEDEAN: story bible

## The point

We are creating a protected innovation capability because our existing way of working makes even small experiments exhausting. The problem is capacity and process: everyone is busy, there are too many handoffs, and the justification needed to do something small is immense. Every team has understandable responsibilities. Nobody is a villain. The combined machinery slowly kills momentum.

## The story in one paragraph

A clean-shaven PM throws away a bad idea (healthy) and picks a good one. Through his window he sees Mount Staedean, 20,000 m, covered in checkpoints. At the first booth he explains his idea at length, takes ticket 087 while the display says 086, and waits until calendar pages fly and stubble grows. He climbs past Customer Success, the Product Owner and Planning, and is told to take a seat each time. He reaches a fork: the ERP roadmap (a dragon) or the AI route (a sea monster). Both lead to the same QA booth. Ancient, bearded and on oxygen, he crawls to the summit and offers the nearly dead bulb to the customer, who asks "Who are you?". Silence. The bulb goes out. End card: "Some ideas should die. This one didn't have to."

## Continuity rules

- **The bulb:** once chosen, it blazes at full brightness until the first checkpoint, then only ever gets dimmer. It goes out on the summit.
- **Bulb hand:** whenever he holds it in one hand, it is his left hand (screen-right when he faces right).
- **Paperwork:** always on the other side, growing from a folder to a stack to a backpack.
- **Beard:** clean-shaven until the first bench, then it only grows, greying from the climb onwards, and drags on the ground at the end.
- **Eyes:** heavy lids never hide the pupils.
- **Display numbers:** 086, then 087, then 088. Later booths show 412.

## The PM (design)

Tousled brown hair, normal nose (the first big-nosed version was rejected), pale-blue shirt with rolled-up sleeves, Tangerine Orange tie, Galaxy Black trousers, brown shoes, pink cheeks.

## The customer

Blond quiff, Sapphire Blue jacket with lapels, burgundy tie, phone in one hand, takeaway coffee with a kraft-brown sleeve in the other. The customer must not wear STAEDEAN colours on the tie or cup.

## Look (final: felt-tip marker)

- White paper, thin black pen line with small breaks.
- Pastel colour that is stronger at the edges of shapes and fades to white in the middle. Light comes from the top left, so shading sits bottom-right.
- Faint streaky marker texture. Pink cheeks.
- Lines shimmer slightly (seed changes every other drawing).
- The bulb is always the brightest thing on screen.

## Sound design

- Office: cheerful plucked theme. It stops dead when the mountain appears.
- Mountain reveal: a riser into three huge hits (dun... dun... DUN) with brass, choir, sub boom and a long reverb tail, plus a faint eagle.
- Voices: muffled "wah-wah" trombone speech (Peanuts-style adults). The customer's line rises like a question.
- Running gags: the ticket printer that whirrs too long; the bench creak plus ticking clock every time he sits; crickets while he waits; a ding-dong when the number changes.
- The climb: plodding bassoon, wind building to a blizzard that cuts to silence at the summit.
- Summit: a calm choir, a hopeful music-box tune that cuts off before "Who are you?", one cricket in the silence, the bulb fizzling out, and two last music-box notes.
